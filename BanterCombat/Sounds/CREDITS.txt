Announcer pack shipped with BanterCombat
========================================

Primary cues from the Dota 2 default announcer / killing-spree lines
© Valve Corporation

Reference:
  https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Announcer_responses

Files:
  FirstBlood, DoubleKill, TripleKill, UltraKill, Rampage,
  KillingSpree, Dominating, MegaKill, Unstoppable, WickedSick,
  MonsterKill, Godlike, HolyShit, Ownage

Playback rules in BanterCombat 1.0:
  - First Blood: once per login session; resets when entering a battleground
  - Multi-kill window (12s): Double → Triple → Ultra → Rampage
  - Revenge kill (and streak 6): Ownage
  - Streak ladder: Killing Spree (3) → Dominating (4) → Mega Kill (5)
    → Ownage (6) → Wicked Sick (7) → Monster Kill (8) → Godlike (9)
    → Holy Shit (10+)
  - Unstoppable.ogg remains for /banter announce and custom packs

These audio files are not licensed by the BanterCombat author.
For redistribution that forbids third-party game audio, delete the .ogg
files and point Options → sound folder at your own pack (see Packs/README.txt).
